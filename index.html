AI Music Publisher

AI Music Publisher is a personal project that helps organize and prepare original music releases for social media.

The project assists with preparing music videos, captions, metadata, and publishing schedules for supported platforms.

Contact

For questions about this project, contact the project owner through GitHub.

Privacy Policy

This website does not collect personal information through forms or use cookies for tracking.

The publishing application may connect to third-party platforms when its owner authorizes those connections. Those platforms handle account information according to their own privacy policies.

Terms of Use

This project is provided for managing and publishing original music content. Users are responsible for ensuring they have the rights to the content they publish and for complying with the rules of connected platforms.
