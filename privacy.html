It is designed to help organize music releases and prepare content for publishing platforms. ## 2. Third-Party Authorization If TikTok integration is implemented, users may be redirected to TikTok to authorize access. The application will request only the permissions needed for its stated features. TikTok handles authorization according to its own privacy policy. ## 3. Information Use Basic TikTok profile information may be used to identify the account authorized to connect to the application. Information required to upload content will be used for that purpose. ## 4. Storage and Sharing The application should only store information necessary for its functionality. Data handling depends on the implemented integration and configuration. Information may be processed by third-party services when users choose to connect them. ## 5. Security and Revoking Access Users should protect their account credentials and authorization tokens. Users can revoke the application's access through the relevant third-party platform's settings. ## 6. Changes to This Policy This policy may be updated as the application develops. ## 7. Contact For privacy questions, contact the project owner through the GitHub repository where this website is hosted.
